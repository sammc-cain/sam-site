A Walk Along the River

8 October 2026

The rain had finally stopped by the time I decided to leave the house. The sky was still grey, but a narrow strip of blue had appeared above the hills, and the air had that fresh smell that always seems to follow a heavy shower.

I took the old path down towards the river, expecting to see very few people. Instead, I passed a couple walking their dog, a cyclist who gave me a cheerful wave, and an elderly man sitting quietly on a bench watching the water.

The river was moving much faster than usual because of the rain. Small branches and leaves were being carried downstream, occasionally catching against the stones before being swept away again.

I stopped on the bridge for a few minutes and watched the water. From there I could see the whole valley, with patches of sunlight appearing between the clouds and lighting up different parts of the landscape.

On the way home I noticed a small wooden sign beside the path that I had somehow never seen before. It pointed towards a narrow trail disappearing into the trees.

I followed it for a few minutes and discovered a clearing with an old stone bench overlooking the river. It was completely hidden from the main path, and for a moment I felt as though I had discovered a secret place that nobody else knew about.

I sat there for a while, listening to the water and the birds in the trees. Eventually the clouds began to gather again, so I decided it was probably time to head home.

By the time I reached the house, it had started raining again.
